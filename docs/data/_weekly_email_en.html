What mattered this week
 
🏛️ Government control of releases
The White House asked OpenAI to delay GPT-5.6 to a small set of government-approved partners, while reportedly clearing xAI to ship freely. AI release decisions are now political, not just technical.
🧬 Model theft and open weights
Anthropic accused Alibaba of a massive distillation attack using 25,000 fake accounts, even as open models like GLM-5.2 narrow the gap. The fight over who owns model intelligence is intensifying.
🏃 AI talent moves
Google DeepMind lost Noam Shazeer to OpenAI and Nobel laureate John Jumper to Anthropic, with more departures reported. The race for elite researchers has become its own front.
