# Traffic & Growth — the honest strategy

_Last updated: 2026-06-22. Supersedes the framing in `SEO_GROWTH.md` (2026-05-18) and
extends `SEO_GOOGLE_NEWS_PLAN.md`. This is the "why traffic is low and what actually
moves it" doc — the strategic backlog lives in `ROADMAP.md`._

## The painful truth

GSC (28-day, as of 2026-06-20): **~5.7k impressions · 17 clicks · 0.3% CTR · avg position ~10 · Search Appearance = "No data"** (zero Top Stories slots).

The problem is **not** that our topics are cold. It's three structural things:

1. **Hot news is the *worst* SEO bet for a low-authority site.** Publishing "OpenAI ships
   GPT-5.5" puts us head-to-head with Reuters / The Verge / TechCrunch — DA 90+, who broke
   it first and own the Top Stories carousel. A ~3-month-old, AI-generated site cannot win
   those head terms via blue links. Google parks us at position ~10 (page-1 bottom / page 2),
   where CTR is ~1%. **Topic heat works against us, not for us.**
2. **Most impressions aren't human demand.** Our top page is `/tools/`, ranking for niche
   model tokens (`lfm2.5`, `deepseek-v4-pro-nvfp4`) and `-site:facebook.com…` operator
   queries — that's **Perplexity / SearchGPT retrieving us (GEO traction)**, not people typing.
3. **We consume the entire AI firehose but distribute ourselves nowhere.** No own social
   presence, no newsletter, no syndication. The best daily AI briefing with zero distribution
   = zero traffic. **Google organic is the slowest, hardest, most authority-gated channel —
   and it's the only one we use.**

Technical SEO is **maxed** (SEO score 100, schema, sitemaps, hreflang, IndexNow, vendor
hubs shipped 2026-06-21). More hygiene will not move the needle. The lever is **distribution +
content types a new site can actually rank for** — not more optimization.

## What actually moves traffic — ranked by payoff / effort

### Tier 1 — distribution (traffic *now*, no authority gate)
- **Be our own publisher on social.** Auto-post the daily briefing / top story to **X,
  LinkedIn, and a Telegram/WhatsApp channel**. AI-hungry audiences live there; a sharp daily
  thread drives direct clicks immediately. The pipeline already produces the content — it
  needs a publish step. (See also the "publishing-as-a-service" idea in ROADMAP.)
- **Hebrew is our single biggest unfair advantage.** Israeli AI-news demand is real and
  *underserved* (Geektime ≈ only serious player). Far less competition in Google-HE **and**
  in Israeli LinkedIn / Facebook groups. We already have an `israeli-social-content` skill.
- **Email newsletter / weekly digest** — the only *compounding, owned* audience. Stops
  traffic being rented from Google. Phased plan already in ROADMAP.

### Tier 2 — content a new site CAN rank for (durable organic)
- **Evergreen comparison / guide pages, not news**: "Claude Opus 4.8 vs GPT-5.5",
  "best AI coding tools 2026", "DeepSeek vs Llama pricing". Steady volume, low
  freshness-competition, focused new site can rank. The **vendor hubs (shipped 06-21)** are
  step one of this entity strategy; comparison pages are the natural next layer.

### Tier 3 — stop flying blind / capture what we get
- **Finish GA4 `outbound_click` Key Event** (pending since 06-15) — we can't see what the
  little traffic does without it.
- **RSS feed** so Feedly / aggregators pull us automatically.

## The mindset shift

SEO is a 6–12 month slow burn we've already front-loaded. To get **clicks this month** we
need **distribution**, and the **Hebrew + social** angle is wide open. Build owned channels
(social, email) that don't depend on Google's authority gate; let SEO compound underneath.
