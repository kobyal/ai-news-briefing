# Scoring rubric (shared — Ari owns it, Maya & Noa read it)

> One rubric, used everywhere, so "score" means the same thing on every card and the BOARD.
> Ari scores. Maya self-screens against it before writing a card. Noa reads the riskiest
> assumption it surfaces. Don't fork this into an agent file — link to it.

## The two gates (must pass BOTH to be scored at all)
1. **Does it work?** — real, *demonstrated* pain + active demand (someone is already searching,
   complaining, or paying). No evidence → fails Gate 1 → kill. Assumption ≠ evidence.
2. **Is it real money?** — a plausible buyer, a price they'd pay, and time-to-first-dollar in
   **weeks, not quarters**. "Could monetize later" → fails Gate 2 → kill or park.

A card that fails either gate is **killed** (with reason) — it is never scored or promoted.

## The 6 criteria (score each 1–5, then weight)

| Criterion | Weight | 1 (weak) | 3 (real) | 5 (strong) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Demand** | ×3 | vibes / "AI will disrupt X", no one asking | a few clear signals (threads, search volume) | crowded demand: people paying competitors, high-volume searches, loud complaints |
| **Monetizability** | ×3 | no buyer or "ads someday" | a buyer + a plausible price | proven willingness-to-pay at a real price point in this niche |
| **Fit with Koby's assets** | ×2 | needs assets Koby lacks | uses 1 asset (site, data, HE edge, pipeline) | compounds 2+ assets (e.g. HE niche × the news-data moat) |
| **Time-to-first-dollar** | ×2 | quarters / needs an audience first | a few weeks | days — can charge or capture demand almost immediately |
| **Defensibility** | ×1 | trivially copyable | small moat (data, trust, HE) | real moat (unique data, brand, switching cost) |
| **Evidence/confidence** | ×1 | guesses, dead links | claims spot-checked, mostly hold | every number independently verified |

**Weighted max = 60.** (3+3+2+2+1+1 = 12 weight units × 5.)

## Thresholds (Ari applies)
- **≥ 42** and both gates pass → **promote** to `validated/` (strong bet).
- **30–41** → **park** as `raw` with a note on the single thing that would push it over (usually
  one more piece of demand evidence). Re-score next room if that evidence appears.
- **< 30**, or either gate fails → **kill** with a reason.

> Score is a *forcing function*, not gospel. Ari may override with a one-line written rationale
> (e.g. "27 but the HE-data moat is rare enough to test cheaply") — the override must be explicit.

## Kill-reason taxonomy (use one; it prevents re-litigation)
`no-demand` · `no-buyer` · `too-slow-to-revenue` · `no-asset-fit` · `not-defensible` ·
`unverifiable-claims` · `regulatory/risk` · `already-tried` (link the prior card) · `out-of-scope`.
